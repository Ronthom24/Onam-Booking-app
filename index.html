Good to know

Questions

How do I book?

Tap Book Tickets, choose how many you need and what you'd like to contribute, and enter your name and mobile number. You'll then pay by UPI — scan the QR or tap through to GPay, PhonePe or any UPI app — and send us the reference number along with a screenshot of the payment. We'll give you a booking ID straight away.

Can I book for my whole family?

Yes. There's no cap on numbers — use the counter in the booking panel to take as many tickets as your family needs in one booking. One payment and one booking ID cover everyone.

What if I lose my ticket?

There's no printed ticket, so there's nothing to lose. Your booking sits against your name and mobile number, so just tell a Santhome Youth volunteer at the desk and we'll find you. If you'd like to check beforehand, call one of the numbers at the bottom of this page.

When should I come?

The main program opens at 10:30 AM with the games, followed by stage events at 11:00 and the sadya from 12:30.

How much should I contribute?

There's no fixed amount — give whatever feels right, and it goes towards the sadya and the day's costs. If you're able to give more, it helps us cover someone who can't. Nobody is turned away over the amount.

How does the lucky draw work?

Every ticket goes into the draw automatically — there's nothing extra to buy and nothing extra to fill in. Book four tickets and you have four chances. We'll draw the winner live on stage during the program, and the winner needs to be there to claim the gold coin.

Do children need tickets?

Anyone eating a sadya needs a ticket, so we know how many leaves to lay. Small children sharing a plate with a parent don't need one.

Is the amount refundable?

The contribution goes towards food that's ordered in advance, so we generally can't refund it. If your plans change, let us know as early as you can — call one of the numbers below and we'll do our best to help.

Thank you for being part of our parish family. We can't wait to share the leaf with you.
